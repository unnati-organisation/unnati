name: Ms. Lata Kachhawaha
---

Ms. Lata Kachhawah is a distinguished social reformer, institution builder, and advocate of women’s empowerment, who has dedicated over four decades to the upliftment of marginalized communities across western Rajasthan.
Through SURE, she has transformed the lives of thousands of women by promoting sustainable livelihoods, traditional handicrafts, Self-Help Groups, financial inclusion, and entrepreneurship. Her work extends to disability inclusion, education, healthcare, sustainable agriculture, water conservation, and holistic rural development, benefiting women, artisans, farmers, children, and vulnerable families across the Thar region.
Driven by compassion, resilience, and an unwavering commitment to grassroots development, Ms. Kachhawah has created lasting institutions and opportunities that promote dignity, self-reliance, and social inclusion. Her lifelong dedication and personal sacrifice stand as an inspiring example of transformative leadership, social justice, and nation-building.
